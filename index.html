Why train here

Everything you need to train.

Designed for regular training — whether your goal is strength, fitness or consistency.

🏋️

Strength Training

Dedicated weight-training space and modern gym equipment for your daily workouts.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Build your foundation with regular resistance training and progressive workouts. The dedicated weight area gives you space for strength-focused sessions. You can work different muscle groups through a structured routine. Consistent strength training can support muscle development, endurance and overall fitness. Ask the trainer for help choosing exercises that match your current level.

🏃

Cardio Zone

Treadmill and cycling options to support your cardio and conditioning sessions.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Use the cardio area for warm-ups, conditioning or dedicated cardiovascular sessions. Treadmill work can be adjusted to your preferred walking or running pace. Cycling gives you another option for steady-state training. Cardio can also be added between strength sessions when you want to increase overall activity. Start at a comfortable intensity and build gradually.

🎯

Personal Training

Personal training options for members who want additional guidance and structure.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Personal training is useful when you want a more structured approach to your workouts. A trainer can help you understand exercise technique and organize your sessions. Your routine can be adjusted around your current fitness level and goals. Guidance can also help you stay consistent and make better use of your training time. Speak with the gym team to discuss the available personal-training options.

🥊

Boxing & Punching Bags

Dedicated punching bags for boxing practice, conditioning, footwork and high-intensity training.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Our new punching bags add boxing-based training to the gym. Bag work can be used for combinations, movement, conditioning and high-intensity rounds. It is also a practical way to add variety to a regular strength workout. Focus on controlled technique, footwork and proper form rather than simply hitting harder. Ask the gym team about using the boxing area safely and effectively.

💧

RO Water

RO purified drinking water available at the gym for your training sessions.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Hydration is an important part of any training session. RO purified drinking water is available at the gym for members. Keeping water nearby makes it easier to maintain regular hydration while exercising. Bring your bottle and refill it when needed. Especially during longer or more intense sessions, remember to take suitable water breaks.

🥤

Supplements

Fitness supplements and accessories available at the gym's supplement shop.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

The gym has a supplement shop with selected fitness products and accessories. Available products can include creatine, pre-workout, protein products, gainers, BCAA and shakers. Stock, flavours and prices can change, so members can ask at the front desk for current availability. Supplements should complement a consistent training and nutrition routine rather than replace it. Ask the gym team for the products currently in stock.

💪

Cupping Therapy

Cupping therapy is available as an additional service at the gym.

CLICK TO LEARN MORE ↓

Cupping therapy is available as an additional service at JM Kalshyan Fitness. Members interested in the service can ask the gym team about availability and the process. It can be considered as an optional part of a broader recovery routine. Individual suitability can vary, so discuss any concerns with an appropriate qualified professional. The gym can provide details about the service currently offered.
